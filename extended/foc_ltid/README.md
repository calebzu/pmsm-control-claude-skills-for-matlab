# FOC-LTID — Load-Torque Estimator + Feed-Forward (reference)

Not a formal skill in this repository. It builds a
field-oriented PMSM speed controller augmented with a linear **load-torque estimator**
(Luenberger + Kalman-filter family, 7 variants) whose estimate is fed forward into the
q-axis current reference to cut load-step speed dips.

## Status & provenance

- **Classical methods** — the 7 estimator variants and the feed-forward law are textbook
  control (Lorenz & Van Patten 1991, Simon *Optimal State Estimation*, Ogata, Anderson &
  Moore). **No new control theory is claimed.**
- **Generated by Claude Code** following this repository's
  `workflow/reference_model_learning_workflow.md`, then **independently reproduced & verified
  by Claude Code** (14/14 Phase-8 cells, byte-identical isolated reproduction; FF-on-vs-off
  dip reduction confirmed on both the equation-linear and Simscape plants).
- The macro closed-loop behaviour (speed tracking + load-dip reduction) was **visually
  confirmed on the MATLAB desktop**.
- The state-estimation theory (F2–F10) is cross-checked against the cited academic sources;
  it has **not been independently reviewed by a domain expert.**
